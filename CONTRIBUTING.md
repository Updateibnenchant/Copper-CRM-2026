## Features

- Contact and company management with customizable tags, notes, and activity history
- Visual sales pipeline with drag-and-drop deal stages and progress tracking
- Task and reminder scheduling linked to contacts and open opportunities
- Built-in reporting dashboard with revenue forecasts and team performance metrics
- Email integration and import/export support for CSV and spreadsheet data
